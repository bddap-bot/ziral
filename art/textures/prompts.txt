TOOL SETTINGS
Codex built-in image tool, default settings, invoked by gen.sh. Every prompt is sent as: "Generate exactly one square image with the image generation tool, then stop: do not judge, retry, edit, or describe it, and write no files. Prompt: <recorded prompt, with [STYLE LOCK] expanded verbatim> No labels, watermark, menus, clutter, or named-game resemblance." Outputs are resized to 1024x1024 and quantised with ImageMagick and pngquant. The grout template grout.png used hex-scaffold.png as the sole edit target; tiles use grout.png as theirs and were generated by 24 independent executions of one identical prompt. When the kiln fires a tile it replaces everything beyond the scaffold stroke's inner edge with grout.png, so a tile's own painted border is never shown. Machines are generated through art/machines/gen.sh from art/machines/manifest.toml.

STYLE LOCK (included verbatim in every prompt)
Ethos: Fired Workshop treats the board as a tabletop instrument assembled from glazed ceramic, darkened brass, and soft rubber. Weight, wear, and warm raking light make each action tactile while colored glazes keep states unmistakable. Palette and roles: board #D8C3A5 clay; arm #6B4F3A dark brass; closed hand #C8553D terracotta; atom kinds #4F8A8B blue-green and #E0A458 amber; glyph #7D5BA6 plum; product #F4EDE4 ivory.

ROTATION DECISIONS
Tiles never rotate: one fixed capture orientation preserves their soft consistent light while the coordinate hash chooses the batch. Bonds rotate to six orientations. Atoms move with rotating arms. Every rotatable shipped texture therefore requests even, diffuse, non-directional illumination with no cast shadow or directional highlight. Curvature on the atom is described symmetrically. Machines are lit at runtime through a normal map (art/MACHINES.md).
