TOOL SETTINGS
Codex built-in image tool, default settings, invoked by gen.sh. Every prompt is sent as: "Generate exactly one square image with the image generation tool, then stop: do not judge, retry, edit, or describe it, and write no files. Prompt: <recorded prompt, with [STYLE LOCK] expanded verbatim> No labels, watermark, menus, clutter, or named-game resemblance." Outputs are resized to 1024x1024 and quantised with ImageMagick and pngquant. Tiles used hex-scaffold.png as the sole edit target and were generated by 24 independent executions of one identical prompt. Machine proposals used the named concept image as the sole edit target.

ROTATION DECISIONS
Tiles rotate through six hashed turns. Bonds rotate to six orientations. The arm rotates and swings. Glyphs are placeable in six turns. Atoms move with rotating arms. Every shipped texture therefore requests even, diffuse, non-directional illumination with no cast shadow or directional highlight. Curvature on the atom is described symmetrically. Machine proposals share the same illumination so their selected sprites may rotate.
