# Molt

A portal frame grown from a curled translucent shrimp carapace, hot coral pink segments, tiny pearly legs tucked around a square black opening, one shed split shell curling outward like a broken parenthesis. Unmistakably a strange biological exoskeleton, not a ceramic ring. Square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The square black central aperture is half the object body width, empty and unobstructed. The whole object including outlying parts is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (coral pink); Materials §2 (translucent chitin); Silhouette §2 (split organic shell rather than complete housing).
