# Mercury spill

A portal frame made of a puddle of liquid mercury with one intensely yellow sulphur crust at its edge. An asymmetrical low liquid shape curves around a perfectly square black dry opening, with three detached satellite droplets. Sharp reflected white streaks in silver. An unstable spill, not an assembled object. Square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The square black central aperture is half the object body width, empty and unobstructed. The whole object including outlying parts is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (silver and sulphur yellow); Materials/light §2 (liquid mirror and sharp reflections); Silhouette §2 (puddle and detached droplets).
