# Hex crust

A portal frame of interdimensional mould that grew along the six edges of a hexagonal hole. The opening is a slightly irregular hexagon with one corner pointing straight up and one straight down, its left and right edges vertical, corners softened. The rim is a crust of pale bone-grey chitin plates overlapping like woodlouse segments, laid in a thin band along each edge. A soft grey-violet mould bloom furs the seams and gathers at the six corners, where clusters of small jointed legs sprout at wrong angles: some splayed outward gripping nothing, some curled inward over the lip. Seen from directly above, straight down, as a square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The frame is low profile: a thin rim, its band about one tenth of the object width, around a large opening that fills most of the object. The opening is the same flat pure green #00FF00 as the background, so the background shows straight through it; nothing lies inside the opening. The whole object, including every leg, is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (bone grey and violet); Materials §2 (chitin and mould); Silhouette §2 (thin rim rather than complete housing); portal preamble and §4 (hexagonal aperture, low-profile frame).
