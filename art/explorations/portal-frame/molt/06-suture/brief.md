# Suture

A portal frame of carapace that split along a Y-shaped moulting suture and peeled open. The opening is a rounded triangle. Three thin flaps of coral-pink shell lie folded back flat around it, each with a ragged torn edge. The pale undersides of the flaps are furred with grey-green mould, and from that mould small jointed legs grow at uneven intervals, a few clustered together, some bent the wrong way at the knee. Seen from directly above, straight down, as a square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The frame is low profile: a thin rim, its band about one tenth of the object width, around a large opening that fills most of the object. The opening is the same flat pure green #00FF00 as the background, so the background shows straight through it; nothing lies inside the opening. The whole object, including every leg, is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (coral pink and grey-green); Materials §2 (split carapace and mould); Silhouette §2 (peeled flaps rather than complete housing); portal preamble and §4 (triangular aperture, low-profile frame).
