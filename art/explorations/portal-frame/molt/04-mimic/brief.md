# Mimic

A portal frame of blue-grey velvet mould that is trying to become an animal. The opening is a clean circle. Along the thin, soft mould rim the substance has formed half-finished arthropod parts: translucent amber husk segments that fade back into fuzz halfway along, stubby legs with too many joints or none, a pair of tiny antennae with no head between them. Nothing is complete; every part dissolves back into velvet mould. Seen from directly above, straight down, as a square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The frame is low profile: a thin rim, its band about one tenth of the object width, around a large opening that fills most of the object. The opening is the same flat pure green #00FF00 as the background, so the background shows straight through it; nothing lies inside the opening. The whole object, including every leg, is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (blue-grey and amber); Materials §2 (velvet mould and husk); Silhouette §2 (dissolving parts rather than complete housing); portal preamble and §4 (circular aperture, low-profile frame).
