# Seam

A portal frame shaped like a moulting seam that split open. The opening is a tall almond with pointed ends at top and bottom. Two thin lips of glossy coral-pink carapace peel back from the split. Along both inner edges a dense comb of tiny pale jointed legs lines the lip like eyelashes, uneven in length, a few bent backward. Rust-orange fungal spore dust and fine white threads of hyphae run through the joints between the carapace plates. Seen from directly above, straight down, as a square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The frame is low profile: a thin rim, its band about one tenth of the object width, around a large opening that fills most of the object. The opening is the same flat pure green #00FF00 as the background, so the background shows straight through it; nothing lies inside the opening. The whole object, including every leg, is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (coral pink and rust); Materials §2 (carapace, legs and hyphae); Silhouette §2 (split seam rather than complete housing); portal preamble and §4 (almond aperture, low-profile frame).
