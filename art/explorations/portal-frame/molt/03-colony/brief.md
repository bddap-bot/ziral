# Colony

A portal frame that is a mould colony grown outward from a hole, like a culture spreading across a dish. The opening is an irregular lobed amoeboid shape. The rim is a thin band of concentric growth zones: a wet bruise-violet ring at the lip, a powdery sulphur-yellow zone, and an outer fringe of fine white hyphae feathering onto the green. Small glossy black-brown carapace scales push up through the fuzz like scabs, and a few tiny jointed legs sprout from the mould at random, pointing in no consistent direction. Seen from directly above, straight down, as a square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The frame is low profile: a thin rim, its band about one tenth of the object width, around a large opening that fills most of the object. The opening is the same flat pure green #00FF00 as the background, so the background shows straight through it; nothing lies inside the opening. The whole object, including every leg, is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (violet, sulphur yellow and white); Materials §2 (mould colony); Silhouette §2 (feathered edge rather than complete housing); portal preamble and §4 (amoeboid aperture, low-profile frame).
