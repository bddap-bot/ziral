# Thorn reliquary

A portal frame woven from charcoal-black thorn branches and translucent amber-red insect wings. Four diagonal spikes radiate from a square black central opening, with fine webbing spanning the thorn forks. A delicate skeletal ritual object, sparse and menacing, entirely unlike a solid machine casting. Square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The square black central aperture is half the object body width, empty and unobstructed. The whole object including outlying parts is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.
