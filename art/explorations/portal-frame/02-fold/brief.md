# Impossible fold

A portal frame made of one folded ribbon of polished silver mirror, forming an impossible Escher-like square knot with contradictory over-under connections. Wide folded planes and razor creases, cold sky-blue reflections, a square black central opening. An impossible mathematical object, not machinery. Square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The square black central aperture is half the object body width, empty and unobstructed. The whole object including outlying parts is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (silver and ice blue); Materials §2 (mirror metal); View §2 (impossible perspective and visible side planes).
