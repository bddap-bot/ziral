# Paper wound

A portal frame formed from torn layers of white and scarlet paper, like a wound in a thick stack of blank pages. The square black opening recedes through stepped, irregular torn strata. One loose long paper curl coils outside a corner. Sculptural paper geology, no lettering. Square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The square black central aperture is half the object body width, empty and unobstructed. The whole object including outlying parts is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (scarlet); Materials §2 (torn paper); Footprint §2 (loose curl exceeds the one-tile envelope).
