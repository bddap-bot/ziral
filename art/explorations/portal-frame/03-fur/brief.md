# Living fringe

A portal frame that is a dense shaggy ring of electric ultramarine fur with six fleshy peach eyelids peering inward, black slit pupils and a square black central opening. Lopsided creature with a blunt fringe, uncanny and tactile, no limbs or human features. Square game-art sprite on flat pure green #00FF00 chroma-key background, object centered, no board or floor. The square black central aperture is half the object body width, empty and unobstructed. The whole object including outlying parts is visible with a generous green margin. Rich material detail readable in a small game sprite. No text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, watermark or people. This is an independent visual exploration with no ceramic, palette or housing-style constraints.

Bible departures: Palette §1 (electric ultramarine and peach); Materials §2 (fur and flesh); Body envelope §2 (hairy organism instead of manufactured solid).
