# Field Notes

Field Notes looks drawn, cut, and pinned during an inventor’s working session: graphite construction, colored-pencil state marks, and small paper repairs. Human irregularity warms the board, but repeated symbols and strong value contrast preserve exact game information.

## Palette

- Board — `#F3EBD3` notebook
- Arm — `#3F4A52` graphite
- Closed hand — `#D95D39` vermilion
- Atom kinds — `#3A8D8F` teal / `#D9A441` ochre
- Glyph — `#7656A8` purple pencil
- Product — `#FFFDF5` white paper

## Rules

**Shape language:** compass circles, ruled links, cut-paper hands, repeated stencil glyphs. Wobble lives at edges, not in placement.

**Material and light:** fibrous paper, graphite grain, colored pencil, occasional tape and erasure. Diffuse daylight; shallow paper shadows only.

**Motion feel:** ticks redraw through two or three ghosted construction poses, then land on a firm line.

**Refuses:** no childish doodles; no illegible handwriting for state; no sepia wash over colors.

## At a glance

- Board: pale penciled hex page.
- Arm: dark ruled graphite link.
- Atom: teal or ochre paper disc.
- Glyph: purple stencil mark.
- Output: clean white cut-paper target.
