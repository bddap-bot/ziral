# Signal Print

Signal Print makes every game state read like a crisp instructional screenprint: broad quiet fields, decisive silhouettes, and a single hot registration accent. Imperfect ink edges add life without weakening the strict visual hierarchy.

## Palette

- Board — `#F2E8D5` warm paper
- Arm — `#17324D` navy
- Closed hand — `#E4572E` orange-red
- Atom kinds — `#2A9D8F` teal / `#E9C46A` gold
- Glyph — `#6C5CE7` violet
- Product — `#F7F7F2` near-white

## Rules

**Shape language:** circles carry matter; straight bars carry reach and bonds; faceted marks carry glyph intent. Use broad outlines and generous gaps.

**Material and light:** matte ink on lightly toothed paper. Flat color only; misregistration may fringe an edge by a hair, never blur a state.

**Motion feel:** each tick snaps like a new printed frame, eased only at the start and finish.

**Refuses:** no gradients; no hairline-only distinction; no decorative marks inside atoms.

## At a glance

- Board: quiet paper hex field.
- Arm: navy pivot and rigid bar.
- Atom: solid teal or gold disc.
- Glyph: isolated violet geometric mark.
- Output: near-white target with a heavy navy rim.
