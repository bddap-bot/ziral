Use case: illustrated manual page. Asset type: one full-page spread from the Fired Workshop's own instrument handbook, square. The supplied image is the page with seven enamelled tokens already pressed into it: leave every token exactly where it is, its size, colours, letter and mark unchanged, and build the page around them. The page is a sheet of thick glazed clay, warm and slightly uneven, framed by a brass border with corner fittings, with brass-wire tendrils and fired-clay flourishes that curl between the tokens so each token sits in its own cartouche as part of the ornament. To the right of each token, in the empty space, paint one small pictorial vignette in the same materials, and nothing else, as explanation: beside the top-left token a terracotta rubber horseshoe hand closing on a blue-green glazed bead; beside the top-right token the same hand open with the bead falling free below it; beside the second-row left token a dark brass arm on a round pivot swung counterclockwise with a curved arrow around the pivot; beside the second-row right token the same arm swung clockwise; beside the third-row left token two blue-green beads joined by a brass bar turning counterclockwise about a horseshoe hand that grips one of them, the arm still; beside the third-row right token the same turning clockwise; beside the bottom token a small brass hourglass at rest. Absolutely no letters, words, numerals, labels or captions anywhere on the page beyond the seven tokens as supplied. Straight-on orthographic view, even diffuse light, no perspective, no cast shadow.
