# Fired Workshop

Fired Workshop treats the board as a tabletop instrument assembled from glazed ceramic, darkened brass, and soft rubber. Weight, wear, and warm raking light make each action tactile while colored glazes keep states unmistakable.

## Palette

- Board — `#D8C3A5` clay
- Arm — `#6B4F3A` dark brass
- Closed hand — `#C8553D` terracotta
- Atom kinds — `#4F8A8B` blue-green / `#E0A458` amber
- Glyph — `#7D5BA6` plum
- Product — `#F4EDE4` ivory

## Rules

**Shape language:** stout circular pivots, one-piece links, open horseshoe hands, inset glyph channels. Silhouettes stay simple despite surface detail.

**Material and light:** glazed clay board and atoms, rubbed brass arms, matte rubber hands. One warm raking light; wear gathers at contact edges only.

**Motion feel:** ticks have weighted starts and soft, decisive seats.

**Refuses:** no exposed clockwork; no grime over state colors; no tiny ornamental hardware.

## At a glance

- Board: pale glazed hex tiles.
- Arm: dark brass pivot and link.
- Atom: colored ceramic bead.
- Glyph: plum glaze inset into clay.
- Output: ivory ceramic cup with brass rim.
