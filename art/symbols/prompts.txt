TOOL SETTINGS
Codex built-in image tool, default settings, three candidates per prompt, generated by art/textures/gen.sh -o art/symbols -n 3 -s 512 into art/symbols/candidates/<key>-<n>.png; the pick is copied to art/symbols/<key>.png and named below. Every prompt is sent by gen.sh as: "Generate exactly one square image with the image generation tool, then stop: do not judge, retry, edit, or describe it, and write no files. Prompt: <the recorded prompt> <the STYLE LOCK verbatim> No labels, watermark, menus, clutter, or named-game resemblance." The image is then resized to 512x512 and quantised (magick, pngquant --quality 70-95).
