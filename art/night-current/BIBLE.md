# Night Current

Night Current turns the board into a quiet field of light where function is encoded by glow color, line weight, and pulse rhythm. Darkness is spacious rather than murky; every active edge remains crisp and readable.

## Palette

- Board — `#07111F` midnight
- Arm — `#24445C` steel blue
- Closed hand — `#FF5A5F` coral
- Atom kinds — `#21D4C2` aqua / `#FFD166` yellow
- Glyph — `#A76DFF` ultraviolet
- Product — `#EAFBFF` ice

## Rules

**Shape language:** thin hex channels, solid arm masses, open hand brackets, concentric atom rings, spare geometric glyphs.

**Material and light:** matte dark surfaces with narrow emissive seams. Bloom never exceeds half a line width; cool rim light separates inactive arms.

**Motion feel:** light leads each tick, the solid form follows, then the pulse settles.

**Refuses:** no fog; no lens flare; no glow as the only silhouette.

## At a glance

- Board: near-black hex field.
- Arm: steel-blue solid bar.
- Atom: bright aqua or yellow disc.
- Glyph: ultraviolet circuit mark.
- Output: ice-white ring that fills on delivery.
