# ziral — design plan

A hypothesis, not a spec. Short, living, rewritten after the first playable.

## Pitch

One sentence: who the player is, what they do, why that is fun.

## Core loop

The 30-second cycle and the interesting decision inside it.
What the player does, never what the game has.

## Feel

Two or three reference games and what to steal from each: camera, pace, input device.

## First playable

The smallest graybox that tests whether the loop is fun. Days, not weeks.
What result would falsify the pitch?

## Out of scope

Explicit list of what the first playable will not have.

## Parking lot

Ideas that are not in the plan.

## Open questions

What is unknown, and which prototype answers each.
